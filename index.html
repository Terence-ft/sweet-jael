About us

About Sweet Jael

Sweet Jael started in a home kitchen and grew into a counter people cross Port Harcourt for. We bake in small batches, fry chin chin the morning it is sold, and blend juices from fruit we would eat ourselves.

The butter is cold, the pepper is not shy, and nothing sits under a lamp all day. Order a box for the house, or pick it up from Port Harcourt GRA.

Tuesday to Sunday, 7:00 to 20:00. Closed on Mondays.
